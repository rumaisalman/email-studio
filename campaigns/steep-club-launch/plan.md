# Campaign plan: The Steep Club launch

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Come and Steep With Us
- **Summary:** A friendly four-week email series that invites our waitlist to join The Steep Club, our monthly tea box.
- **Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription by Sunday 29 November 2026.
- **Dates:** Monday 2 November 2026 to Sunday 29 November 2026.
- **Other goals:**
  - Keep unsubscribes low, so the list stays healthy for future emails.
  - Learn which of our three groups is most likely to subscribe.
  - Collect questions from tea friends to help the next campaign.

300 out of about 2,000 people is 15%. That is a big ask, even for a warm waitlist. These people asked to hear from us, which helps. But we should keep an eye on progress each week (see section 7).

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 people who joined the waitlist. They already like us, so we are past "who are you?" They are deciding whether the box is right for them.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | New to loose-leaf tea. Keen, but not sure where to start. | To try new teas without fuss. Simple steps to brew a good cup. | Worry it's too fancy, or that they'll brew it "wrong". |
| **Gift buyers** | Looking for a thoughtful present for a tea lover. | An easy, lovely gift that arrives each month. | Not sure how gifting works, or when the first box arrives. |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Drink tea every day. Know what they like. | Fresh leaves on a regular schedule, plus new teas to try. | Worry the box won't suit their taste, or that they'll get stuck in a plan. |

**Where they spend time:** their inbox. They asked us for email, so that's where we'll talk to them.

## 3. What we want to say

**Main message:** The Steep Club brings a cosy box of fresh leaves to your door each month, and you're invited.

| Supporting message | Best for | Proof to use |
|---|---|---|
| Every month, a new mix of fresh leaves to steep and enjoy. | Daily tea drinkers | Photos of a real box and the teas inside. |
| Each box comes with simple brew notes, so every cup is easy. | Beginners | A real brew card from the box. |
| A gift that keeps arriving, month after month. | Gift buyers | A clear "how gifting works" note with real dates. |
| You're in charge. Skip or cancel whenever you like. | Everyone | The real skip and cancel steps from our shop. |

**Notes on proof:** only use real photos, real quotes from tea friends and real shop policies. Don't make up reviews or numbers. If we don't have a quote yet, leave it out.

**Brand checks for every email** (from `brand/voice.md`):
- Friendly, warm and welcoming. Short sentences.
- Gentle invitations ("Come and have a look"). Never "hurry", "buy now" or "limited time".
- No health claims, no cure claims, and never "caffeine-free".
- End with "Happy steeping, The Kettlebird team".

## 4. Channel plan

**Email only.** No paid ads, no social posts.

- **Why it fits:** everyone on the list asked to hear from us. Email costs very little and lets us write to each group differently.
- **Format:** short, plain emails with one clear link each. Each email gets one version for each of our three groups.
- **Effort:** low to medium. Six launch emails, three group versions each, plus one welcome email.
- **Sending:** practice server only until the list is checked and approved. Never send to real people from this studio.

## 7. How we'll measure success

**Main measure:** number of new subscriptions from the waitlist. **Target: 300 by 29 November.**

Weekly targets, to keep us on track:

| By the end of | New members | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (8 Nov) | 120 | 120 |
| Week 2 (15 Nov) | 80 | 200 |
| Week 3 (22 Nov) | 50 | 250 |
| Week 4 (29 Nov) | 50 | 300 |

Week 1 is usually the biggest week, because the waitlist is most excited at the start.

**Other measures:**

| Measure | What it tells us | Aim | How we track it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Share of people who open each email | Are the subject lines working? | 40% or more | Email tool report |
| Share of people who click the link | Is the email interesting enough to act on? | 5% or more | Email tool report |
| Unsubscribes per email | Are we sending too much, or sounding pushy? | Under 0.5% | Email tool report |
| Sign-ups by group | Which group likes the box most? | Compare the three groups | Sign-up link tagged by group |
| Replies and questions | What are people unsure about? | Note every question | Shared inbox |

**Reporting:** a short check every Monday. Count the sign-ups, compare them with the weekly target, and change the next email if needed. Write a full wrap-up on Monday 30 November.

## 8. Budget

The budget is small, and this plan spends almost nothing beyond time.

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Email tool | Our existing plan | Check the plan covers 2,000 contacts and about 14,000 sends. |
| Writing and checking emails | Team time | About half a day per email, including group versions. |
| Box photos | Team time | Shoot them at home or in the shop. |
| Spare time | 10 to 15% of team time | For fixing an email that isn't working, or answering questions. |

There's no money for ads. If we later want to offer something extra (like a sample tin with the first box), that's a decision to make before launch. See section 10.

## 9. Risks and how we'll handle them

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **The 15% goal is too high.** We might fall short of 300. | Check every Monday. If week 1 is under 90, change subject lines and the main group message for week 2. Use replies to find out what's holding people back. |
| **Too many emails, so people unsubscribe.** | Stop launch emails as soon as someone joins. Keep week 3 to one email. Stop and rethink if unsubscribes go over 0.5%. |
| **A health claim or pushy word slips into an email.** | Run every email through a brand review before it goes out. Check against `brand/voice.md`, especially section 4. |
| **An email reaches a real person by mistake.** | Use the practice server only. Practice lists use `@example.com` addresses. The send guard blocks anything else. |
| **Gift buyers miss the delivery date for a gift.** | Put the real "order by" date clearly in Emails 3 and 6 once the shop confirms it. |
